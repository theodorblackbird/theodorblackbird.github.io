Théodor Lemerle

I completed my PhD at IRCAM (STMS lab, CNRS / Sorbonne Université) in the Analysis/Synthesis team, supervised by Axel Roebel and Nicolas Obin. My thesis, Toward Expressive and Long-Form Speech Synthesis (defense video), set out to build text-to-speech (TTS) models that can convincingly tell a story to a child. We proposed methods for control of high-level attributes (eg. emotions, prosody) under scarce annotated data regime. We also introduced architectures for stable synthesis with no measurable degradation over hours of speech, while being fully streamable in text and audio.

Before that, I studied acoustics, signal processing and computer science for music (ATIAM) at IRCAM, and mathematics and computer science for machine learning at Sorbonne Université (M2A).

My interests encompass: generative modeling, representation learning, efficient generation (eg. fast, low-latency, streamable) with a particular focus on speech and TTS.
